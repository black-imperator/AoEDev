[desc]
Gives civilizations a small chance to emerge from barbarian cities depending on if they meet certain requirements. Note that this will avoid cities that have the Dragon's Horde, so you shouldn't get a situation where the Clan of Embers will spawn on top of that.

Current Civs and their Emergent Requirements:

CLAN OF EMBERS 
 	- Requires a Barbarian city with at least 4 population
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Crafting technology
	- The Clan of Embers will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Taxation
DOVIELLO
 	- Requires a Barbarian city that is near forests
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Hunting technology
	- The Doviello will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Iron Working
MAZATL
 	- Requires a Barbarian city that is near Mount Kelshekk
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Bronze Working technology
	- Requires that AC is below 20
	- The Mazatl will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Strength of Will
CUALLI
 	- Requires a Barbarian city that is near rainforests
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Bronze Working technology
	- The Cualli will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Taxation
SHEAIM
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Knowledge of the Ether technology and that AC is above 10
	- The Sheaim will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Strength of Will
	- Emergence is re-enabled if AC is greater than 50
MEKARA ORDER
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Knowledge of the Ether technology
	- Requires that the Amurites exist on the map
	- The Mekara Order will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Strength of Will
	- Mekara Order will no longer be able to emerge if the Amurites have Evil alignment
LEGION OF DTESH
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Necromancy technology
	- Requires that the Amurites or Mekara Order exist on the map
	- Dtesh will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Omniscience
LANUN
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Sailing technology
	- Requires that the barbarian city is on the coast
	- The Lanun will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Taxation
ARCHOS
 	- Requires a Barbarian city that is near forests
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Animal Husbandry technology
	- The Archos will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Iron Working
HIPPUS
 	- Requires a Barbarian city that is landlocked and on flat terrain
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Horseback Riding technology
	- The Hippus will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Currency
CALABIM
 	- Requires a Barbarian city with at least 4 population
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Iron Working technology
	- The Calabim will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Guilds
SVARTALFAR
 	- Requires a Barbarian city that is near forests and has the Council of Esus
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Deception technology
	- The Svartalfar will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Guilds
ILLIANS
 	- Requires a Barbarian city that is near Letum Frigus and that AC is above 10
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Mysticism technology
	- Illians will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Omniscience
BALSERAPHS
 	- Requires a Barbarian city that is near the Tower of Eyes
	- Requires that the barbarians have access to the Knowledge of the Ether technology
	- Balseraphs will no longer be able to emerge if barbarians have researched Strength of Will
