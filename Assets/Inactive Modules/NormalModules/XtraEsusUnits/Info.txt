[desc]

Added the Proprietor, Death Merchant, Enforcer, and Whisper as religious units you can build if you have the Council of Esus as your state religion. Each has its own specific use and are meant to add some extra flair to the religion and its themes.

Proprietor:
- Convert Crime into culture
- Steal Culture for your Capital
- Steal GPP for your Capital

Death Merchant:
- Convert Crime into gold
- Boost military production
- Boost Crime

Whisper
- Slightly weaker assassin that can cast shadow spells for themselves and your unit stacks

Enforcer
- Melee unit that gains strength the more Crime a city you own has

Additionally, once you research Guilds, you can spend gold to upgrade your Aphotic Thrones.
